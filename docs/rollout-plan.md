# Moda Rollout: Change Management and Communications Plan

Pilot at a fashion retail shop | Illustrative scenario | Version 1.1 (draft)

> **Status:** illustrative portfolio document. The shop, team size and targets are assumptions, not a real client engagement, and the plan has not been tested with real users.

## 1. Purpose and scenario

This plan shows how a small fashion shop could move from its current way of working to Moda, an AI styling assistant with a Staff Catalog Portal and an in-store Styling Assistant. It covers who is affected, what changes for them, how and when they hear about it, how they are trained, and how success is measured.

Assumptions (illustrative, to be replaced with real shop details): one shop, one owner, one store manager, a small team of sales staff, and one team member who looks after stock. The shop currently tracks stock in a spreadsheet or register and relies on staff memory and colleagues for styling advice. Moda is run as a pilot in one shop only.

## 2. What is changing

| Area | Today | With Moda |
|---|---|---|
| Product records | Spreadsheet or register, with inconsistent detail on colour, formality or season | Staff Catalog Portal: photo upload, AI-suggested tags, staff review, and required SKU, price, stock and sizes |
| Data quality | Mistakes and duplicates found by chance | Validation rejects invalid category pairings, negative prices and duplicate SKUs |
| Styling advice | Depends on which staff member is on shift and what they remember | Styling Assistant gives up to three outfit options from in-stock items |
| Pricing and stock at the point of advice | Checked by hand | Each look shows item prices, sizes, remaining stock and a total bundle price |
| Out-of-stock items | Staff must remember what has sold out | Items with zero stock are excluded automatically |
| Weather | Not considered unless mentioned | Live weather is shown in the Styling Assistant |

## 3. Feature comparison: current way and Moda

| Capability | Spreadsheet or register | Moda |
|---|---|---|
| Record a product | Type a row by hand | Upload a photo; AI suggests tags; staff review and confirm |
| Catch tagging mistakes | Manual checking | Confidence scores, Needs Review flags under 70%, and validation rules |
| Suggest a full outfit | No | Up to three options (dress, separates, layered) |
| Show price and stock while styling | Look up separately | Shown on every look, with a calculated bundle price |
| Avoid sold-out items | Staff must check | Zero-stock items are excluded |
| Account for weather | No | Yes, live weather lookup |
| Needs setup effort | Low | Medium: every item must be catalogued once |

## 4. Stakeholder analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest in the change | Impact / influence | Likely concern | How we engage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shop owner | Better sales and a consistent customer experience | Medium / High | Cost, time, and whether it really sells more | Short briefing on the pilot goals; share adoption results every two weeks |
| Store manager | Smooth daily operations | High / High | Extra work during busy hours | Co-plan the schedule; make the manager the pilot sponsor on the floor |
| Sales and styling staff | Faster, more confident advice | High / Medium | Fear of being replaced or judged by the tool | Position Moda as an assistant; hands-on demo; a champion for peer help |
| Stock and catalogue staff | Accurate stock records | High / Medium | Cataloguing every item takes time, and AI tags may be wrong | Start with best-selling items; teach the review step; staff choices always override the AI |
| Customers | Useful, well-priced suggestions | Medium / Low | Recommendations that do not fit them | Staff stay in the loop and choose what to show |
| System maintainer | A stable pilot | Medium / High | Bugs and slow responses | Weekly issue review; fast fixes for blockers |

## 5. Change impact assessment

| Role | What changes in their day | Impact | Support needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stock and catalogue staff | Upload photos, review AI tags, enter SKU, price, stock and sizes; keep stock counts current | High | Cataloguing job aid, workshop, checklist |
| Sales and styling staff | Use the Styling Assistant with customers and read the look cards | High | Quick-start job aid, hands-on practice, floor support |
| Store manager | Oversees the pilot; reviews adoption and feedback | Medium | Weekly summary of adoption metrics |
| Shop owner | Sponsors the pilot; decides whether to continue | Low | Short briefing and a final results summary |

## 6. Communications plan

The plan follows the pilot go-live date (T). Messages are short, say why the change is happening, and tell people what to do next.

| When | Message | Audience | Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T minus 3 weeks | What Moda is, why the shop is trying it, what the pilot involves and who to ask | All staff | Team meeting plus a short written note | Awareness and the reason for change |
| T minus 2 weeks | Live demo of both screens and open Q and A; introduce the Moda champion | All staff | Team meeting | Reduce fear, answer questions early |
| T minus 1 week | Share the user guide, how to open the portal, and the cataloguing checklist | Staff using the system | Email or group chat; printed job aid at the counter | Readiness |
| T minus 3 days | Reminder: training session time and what to bring | Stock and sales staff | Group chat | Attendance |
| Go-live (T) | Moda is live; the old spreadsheet stays as backup during the pilot | All staff | Morning huddle | Clear start and a safety net |
| T plus 3 days | Quick check-in: what is working, what is confusing | All staff | Short huddle and feedback note | Early support and fixes |
| T plus 2 weeks | Tips from the champion and first adoption numbers | All staff and owner | Group chat and short summary | Reinforce use and show progress |
| T plus 4 weeks | Pilot results, feedback summary and the decision on next steps | All staff and owner | Team meeting | Close the pilot and decide |

## 7. Training plan

| Session | Who | Content | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demo and Q and A | All staff | What Moda does; a live walk-through of both screens | 30 minutes |
| Cataloguing workshop | Stock staff and champion | Upload, review AI tags, fix Needs Review items, enter SKU, price, stock and sizes | 45 minutes, hands-on |
| Styling practice | Sales and styling staff | Building looks for real customer cases and reading the look cards | 30 minutes, hands-on |
| Floor support | Anyone | One-to-one help from the champion during the first week | As needed |

Materials: the [Moda User Guide](user-guide.md) (job aids and FAQ), a one-page printed quick-start at the counter, and a short cataloguing checklist.

## 8. Support after go-live (keeping the lights on)

- A named Moda champion in the shop answers first-line questions.
- A simple issue log records problems, who reported them and whether they are fixed.
- The FAQ and troubleshooting table are updated each week from real questions.
- A short weekly review with the store manager covers issues and adoption numbers.

## 9. Readiness and adoption metrics

Targets below are proposals for the pilot and should be agreed with the shop owner.

| Metric | How it is measured | Proposed target |
|---|---|---|
| Training completion | Attendance list | All pilot staff trained before go-live |
| Catalogue ready | Share of active stock catalogued with SKU, price, stock and sizes | At least 80% of active stock by week 2 |
| AI tag quality | Share of AI tags that staff had to correct | Tracked weekly; should fall as staff learn the review step |
| Needs Review backlog | Items still flagged Needs Review | Cleared within one week |
| Active use | Staff who create recommendations each week | Most staff use it weekly |
| Staff confidence | Two-question survey, 1 to 5 | Average of 4 or more by week 4 |
| Support demand | Number of help requests per week | Falling after week 2 |

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI tags are wrong, so recommendations look wrong | Needs Review flags, a confidence threshold, and staff choices that always override the AI; the champion spot-checks tags in week 1 |
| Stock counts are out of date, so customers are shown unavailable items | Weekly stock check; zero-stock items are excluded automatically |
| Staff prefer the old way or fear being replaced | Position Moda as an assistant; involve staff in the demo; staff choose what to show |
| Duplicate or invalid records | Validation rejects duplicate SKUs, invalid pairings and negative prices; the cataloguing checklist covers the rest |
| Slow responses or an outage of an outside service | The system has timeouts and fallbacks; keep the spreadsheet as a backup during the pilot |
| The system supports a single shop inventory today | Run the pilot in one shop only; treat multi-shop support as a later step |

## 11. Exit criteria for the pilot

- Training and catalogue readiness targets are met.
- Staff confidence and weekly use reach the agreed levels.
- No unresolved blocking issues in the issue log.
- The shop owner confirms whether to continue, adjust or stop.

## 12. Framework alignment: ADKAR

The plan above follows the five stages of the ADKAR model (Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability, Reinforcement), so that each stage of an individual's change journey has a matching activity.

| ADKAR stage | What the person needs | Activities in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | To understand why the change is happening | T minus 3 weeks announcement explaining the reason; team meeting |
| Desire | To want to take part in the change | Live demo and open Q and A; Moda champion; positioning Moda as an assistant, not a replacement; staff review step that keeps people in control |
| Knowledge | To know how to change | User Guide, job aids, FAQ, cataloguing checklist; demo and cataloguing workshop |
| Ability | To be able to do it in practice | Hands-on cataloguing and styling practice; one-to-one floor support in the first week |
| Reinforcement | To keep using it | Check-in at T plus 3 days; tips at T plus 2 weeks; results at T plus 4 weeks; adoption metrics and weekly review |
